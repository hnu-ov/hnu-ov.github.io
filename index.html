Perceiving the unseen in dynamic worlds, enabling AI to adapt, reason, and explore beyond closed-world boundaries.

I am an Associate Professor in the School of Computer Science and Technology at Hainan University. My research centers on Open-World Visual Perception, with broad interests in Zero-Shot Learning, Multimodal Learning and Large Multimodal Models, Embodied AI, and Remote Sensing Image Analysis. I aim to develop intelligent, trustworthy, and adaptable visual systems for complex real-world environments.

I received my Ph.D. degree in Computer Science and Engineering from Beihang University in 2022, where I also received my B.Sc. degree in Mathematical Sciences in 2016. From 2022 to 2024, I was a Lecturer in the School of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence at Wuhan University of Technology.

[Prospective students] Our group welcomes highly motivated Ph.D. and Master's students, visiting students, and undergraduate research interns. If you are interested in our research, please email me your CV and, if available, a brief research statement or representative publications.
